DOI lookup

Nearly every published paper has a DOI: a code like 10.1093/beheco/araa090, printed on the article page and in the journal's citation box. Paste it here and this page finds the paper's details so you can copy them into the Publications form.

Pasting the whole link is fine too, like https://doi.org/10.1093/beheco/araa090; the page trims it for you.

Then, to put it on the site

  1. Open the editor in a new tab and click Publications in the left list, then the New Publication button at the top right.
  2. Come back to this tab. Click Copy next to Title, go to the editor tab, click into the Title box, and paste. If the title has a species name, select it there and click the I button to italicize it.
  3. For each author here, click Add author in the editor, then copy the surname into Surname and the first names into First name(s). Keep the order shown here.
  4. Copy Journal, Year, and DOI into their boxes the same way.
  5. Check your own name: it should read Rittinger as the surname (that spelling is what makes it bold on the site). Fix it if the journal abbreviated it.
  6. Click Save (top right). The site rebuilds in 3 to 7 minutes; the status page watches it for you.
