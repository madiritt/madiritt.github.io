DOI to BibTeX

Nearly every published paper has a DOI: a code like 10.1093/beheco/araa090, printed on the article page and in the journal's citation box. Paste it here and this page writes the BibTeX block for you.

Pasting the whole link is fine too, like https://doi.org/10.1093/beheco/araa090; the page trims it for you.

Then, to put it on the site

  1. Click Copy it above.
  2. Open the editor and click Publications (advanced) in the left list, then the entry inside it.
  3. Click into the big BibTeX box, scroll to the very end, and press Enter twice so there is one blank line after the last block.
  4. Paste.
  5. Check the pasted block: your name should read Rittinger, Madison somewhere in the author line (that spelling is what makes it bold on the site). Fix the spelling if the journal abbreviated it.
  6. Click Save (top right). The site rebuilds in 3 to 7 minutes; the status page watches it for you.
