Professional Summary

Behavioral ecologist and Ph.D. candidate studying the behavioral and cognitive processes of animals, with a focus on decision-making and problem-solving in spiders. Experienced in experimental design, quantitative analysis in R, microCT imaging, and undergraduate teaching and mentorship.
